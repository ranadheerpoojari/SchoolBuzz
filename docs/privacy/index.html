🐝 SchoolBuzz

Privacy Policy · Last updated: September 5, 2026

SchoolBuzz is a personal-use mobile application for coordinating school drop-off and pickup. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Caregiver name — the name you enter during setup (e.g. "Dad", "Mom")
  • Child name — the name of your child
  • School name and coordinates — the school name and GPS coordinates you configure
  • Geofence radius — your configured detection radius (100–500 meters)
  • Schedule settings — active days, drop-off/pickup time windows
  • Event history — drop-off, pickup, and message events you create
  • App preferences — cooldown settings, enabled/disabled state

1.2 Data Collected by Firebase (if enabled)

  • Crash reports — anonymous crash logs and stack traces (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Usage analytics — screen views, feature usage, anonymized events (Firebase Analytics)
  • FCM token — device token for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Synced events — if you enable family sync, events are stored in Cloud Firestore

1.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Continuous GPS location or location history
  • ❌ WhatsApp credentials, messages, or group IDs
  • ❌ Contact lists or address books
  • ❌ Photos, videos, or files
  • ❌ Browsing history
  • ❌ Personal identification documents
  • ❌ Financial or payment information

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Geofencing — Your device's GPS is used only to calculate distance to your configured school. Location is checked every 30 seconds and is never stored or transmitted.
  • Event logging — Drop-off, pickup, and message events are stored locally on your device for your reference.
  • WhatsApp sharing — Messages are generated locally and shared via Android/iOS share sheet. We do not access WhatsApp directly.
  • Crash reporting — Anonymous crash data helps us fix bugs. No personal information is included.
  • Analytics — Anonymized usage data helps us understand which features are used. No personal information is included.
  • Family sync — If enabled, events are synced across your family devices via Cloud Firestore. Only family members you invite can access this data.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. The only external services used are:

  • Google Firebase — for crash reporting, analytics, and optional cloud sync. Data is processed according to Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp — messages are shared via your device's share sheet. We do not interact with WhatsApp's servers directly.

4. Data Security

  • All local data is stored in the app's private sandbox (other apps cannot access it)
  • Firebase data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
  • No server credentials are embedded in the app
  • No WhatsApp credentials are stored

5. Data Retention

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • You can clear event history at any time from the app's History screen
  • Firebase analytics data is retained for 14 months (Google default)
  • Firestore data persists until you delete it

6. Your Rights

  • Access — All your data is visible in the app (Settings, History)
  • Delete — Clear history in-app, or uninstall to remove all local data
  • Export — Event history can be shared via WhatsApp or system share
  • Opt out — Disable Firebase analytics in your device settings

7. Children's Privacy

SchoolBuzz is designed for use by parents and caregivers. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The child's name is stored only on the caregiver's device and is used solely to label school events.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
