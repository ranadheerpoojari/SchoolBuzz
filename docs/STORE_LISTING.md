### Full Description (4000 characters max)
```
🐝 SchoolBuzz — The simplest way to coordinate school drop-off and pickup.

SchoolBuzz automatically detects when you arrive at school and lets you instantly log a drop-off, pickup, or send a custom message to your family WhatsApp group.

✨ KEY FEATURES

📍 Auto-Detection
Set your school location once. SchoolBuzz uses geofencing to detect when you arrive and shows a notification with one-tap actions.

✅ Three Simple Actions
• Drop-off — Log morning arrival
• Pick-up — Log afternoon pickup
• Message — Send a custom update ("Running late", "Waiting outside", etc.)

💬 WhatsApp Sharing
Each action generates a clean, formatted message that opens directly in WhatsApp. Choose your family group and send — that's it.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Multi-Caregiver
Install on Mom's phone, Dad's phone, Grandma's phone. Each device stores its own identity. The WhatsApp group is your shared record.

🔒 Privacy First
• No continuous location tracking
• No data uploaded to servers
• No WhatsApp credentials stored
• All events stay on your device

⏰ Smart Schedule
Configure active days (Mon–Fri, or any combo) and time windows for drop-off and pickup. No notifications outside school hours.

🔁 Duplicate Protection
Configurable cooldown (5–120 minutes) prevents repeated notifications when you're near school.

📝 Event History
View all your past drop-offs, pickups, and messages with timestamps and share status.

🌙 Dark Mode
Automatically follows your device theme.

💰 100% Free
No ads. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases. No sign-up required.

🏫 PERFECT FOR
• Parents doing the school run
• Grandparents helping with pickup
• Babysitters coordinating with families
• Any caregiver who needs to log school visits

📲 How it works:
1. Enter your name, child's name, and school location
2. Drive to school — SchoolBuzz detects your arrival
3. Tap Drop-off or Pickup
4. Share the update to your WhatsApp family group
5. Done!

Manual mode is also available — tap "I'm at School" anytime.

SchoolBuzz works on both Android and iOS with a single account.
```
